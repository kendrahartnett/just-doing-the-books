02 / About Just Doing the Books

About Just Doing the Books

Joanna Plues started this small business in 2012 and has been helping neighbors and local businesses keep their books ever since. She holds a Bookkeeping Certification from Eastern Arizona College, along with two Associate degrees — one in Business Science and one in Administrative Information Services.

With years of experience and a strong attention to detail, Joanna understands how important it is to keep financial records organized, accurate, and up to date. She takes the time to understand each client’s needs and works hard to make bookkeeping feel less stressful and more manageable.

She treats every business and personal bookkeeping need with the same care and respect she would give her own, helping clients stay organized, keep track of their finances, and feel more confident about their financial records.
