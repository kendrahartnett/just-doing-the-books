A one-page marketing site for **Just Doing the Books**, a bookkeeping and tax service run by Joanna Plues, serving personal and small-business clients in Payson, Arizona since 2012.



## Project Background

This site is **Build Project 1** for the Next Chapter Project program. The assignment was to build a simple one-page site for a real purpose using AI assistance — graded not just on whether it works, but on whether the builder stayed in control of the AI throughout.

### Spec (build exactly this, nothing extra)
- A clear headline and one-sentence description of who/what the site is for.
- Exactly three sections of content.
- One way to take action (a button, a link, or contact info).

### Sections on this site
1. **Services** — bookkeeping and tax services offered.
2. **About** — Joanna's background, credentials, and approach.
3. **Get in Touch** — the one call-to-action (email button), with phone/text and accepted payment methods listed as supporting text underneath.

### Constraints this project is graded on
- **Stay in scope** — exactly three sections, no bonus features.
- **Explain every part** — able to point at any piece of the site and say what it does, in plain terms.
- **Log every prompt** — a running record of what was asked of the AI and why, kept as evidence of the thinking behind the build.
- **Catch one mistake** — find and fix at least one thing the AI got wrong or did poorly, and note what it was.

_Known limitation to review: `mailto:` links only open something if the visitor's device has a default email app configured — worth testing on real devices before treating the email button as fully reliable._

### Grading rubric
1. **Build skill** — works and matches the spec.
2. **Control under constraints** — the big one: scope discipline, ability to explain every part, prompt log, caught mistake.
3. **Demo & communication** — a clear 2-minute demo, handling two follow-up questions.
4. **Meets-the-spec** — headline, three sections, one call-to-action.
