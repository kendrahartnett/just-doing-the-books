# Build Project 1 — 2-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:15 — Open
"This is a one-page site I built for my mom's bookkeeping business, Just Doing the Books, in Payson, AZ. The spec was a headline, exactly three sections, and one call-to-action — and the real grade isn't whether it works, it's whether I stayed in control of the AI the whole way through."

## 0:15–1:10 — Walk the live site
(share your screen, scroll top to bottom)

- Headline + one-line description at the top.
- Section 1, Services: "personal and small business bookkeeping, statements, P&L, tax prep, e-filing — this is everything she actually offers, nothing added."
- Section 2, About: her background, certification, two AA degrees, why clients trust her.
- Section 3, Contact: "This is the one call-to-action — the email button. Phone number and payment methods are listed underneath as plain text, not extra buttons, because the spec said exactly one CTA."
- One line on tech: "Plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, nothing fancy, on purpose."

## 1:10–1:45 — The part that's actually graded
"Three things prove I stayed in control here. First, scope: when a fourth section started forming at the bottom of the page, I merged it back into section three instead of letting it become a fourth. Second, I caught three real mistakes the AI made — it added clickable nav tabs that weren't in the spec, and I had to remove them twice because they came back; it moved the wrong content when I asked it to relocate something; and it packaged extra files when I asked for HTML/CSS/JS only. Third, every prompt is logged — I can show you the exact one where I caught each of those."

## 1:45–2:00 — Close
"Everything on this page, I can point to and explain what it does and why it's there — that's the log and the spec doc backing it up."

## Prepping for two questions

**"Why only one CTA when you had three ways to reach her?"**
→ "The spec required exactly one, so I picked email as primary and kept the phone number and payment info as supporting text, not competing buttons."

**"What's the biggest mistake you caught?"**
→ "The AI added navbar tabs that weren't in scope — twice, since they resurfaced after I first removed them. That's in my prompt log as the main caught mistake."

**"What would you change with more time?"**
→ Have a real, honest, small answer ready — e.g. testing the `mailto:` button on more devices.

**"Walk me through this one piece of code."**
